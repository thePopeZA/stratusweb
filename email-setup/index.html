Set up your email

Add your Stratus email to your phone or computer. Pick your device below — it takes a couple of minutes, and you only do it once.

Your device

  • Mac Mail
  • Android

Your Stratus email works with any mail app. You'll need two things: your full email address (like info@yourbusiness.co.za) and the password you set in your Stratus dashboard. If you haven't set a password yet, sign in to your dashboard first and set one under My email.

iPhone & iPad

⚡ The easy way — one tap

Sign in to your Stratus dashboard on your iPhone, go to My email, and tap “Set up on iPhone / Mac (automatic)” next to your address. Your phone sets everything up for you — you just type your password when it asks. That's it.

Prefer to do it by hand? Here's how:

  1. Open Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account.
  2. Tap Other, then Add Mail Account.
  3. Enter your name, your email address, your password, and a description. Tap Next.
  4. At the top, choose POP (not IMAP).
  5. For both Incoming and Outgoing Mail Server, use host name mail.stratusnet.co.za, your full email address as the username, and your password.
  6. Tap Save. If asked about SSL, leave it On.

Mac Mail

On a Mac you can also use the one-tap profile above (download it from your dashboard in Safari). Or set it up by hand:

  1. Open Mail → Mail menu → Add Account.
  2. Choose Other Mail Account and click Continue.
  3. Enter your name, email address and password, then Sign In.
  4. When it can't find the settings automatically, choose POP as the account type.
  5. Incoming and Outgoing mail server: mail.stratusnet.co.za. Username: your full email address.
  6. Click Sign In / Done.

Android

Steps vary slightly by phone, but in the Gmail app (which most Android phones have):

  1. Open Gmail → tap your picture (top right) → Add another account.
  2. Choose Other.
  3. Type your full email address, tap Next, and when asked choose Personal (POP3).
  4. Enter your password, tap Next.
  5. Incoming server: mail.stratusnet.co.za, port 995, security SSL/TLS.
  6. Outgoing server: mail.stratusnet.co.za, port 465, security SSL/TLS, sign-in required, same username and password.
  7. Tap Next / Done.

Outlook (Windows)

  1. Open Outlook → File → Add Account.
  2. Type your email address, then click Advanced options and tick Let me set up my account manually. Click Connect.
  3. Choose POP as the account type.
  4. Incoming mail: server mail.stratusnet.co.za, port 995, This server requires an encrypted connection (SSL/TLS) ticked.
  5. Outgoing mail (SMTP): server mail.stratusnet.co.za, port 465, encryption SSL/TLS.
  6. Click Connect, enter your password, and finish.

All the settings, in one place

Mail server (incoming & outgoing): mail.stratusnet.co.za
Incoming — POP3: port 995, SSL on
Outgoing — SMTP: port 465, SSL on
Username: your full email address
Password: the one you set in your Stratus dashboard
